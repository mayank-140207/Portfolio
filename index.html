01 — About

The case and the build.

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science Engineering student at GLA University with an unusual second job: Chief Strategy Officer at Baoiam Innovations, an EdTech startup. My work there sits across strategic planning, go-to-market design, and cross-functional coordination with Operations, Marketing, Product, Design, and Technology — turning a founder's direction into a plan a team can actually execute.

That business instinct feeds directly into what I build. When I designed MediHintz, a symptom-triage app, I treated it the same way I'd treat a product launch: define the user problem precisely, scope an MVP that ships, and design the escalation logic before the interface. I care about the same thing in both worlds — decisions that are specific, testable, and grounded in what the data actually says, not in what sounds good in a deck.

Outside of Baoiam, I'm a Student Relations Member for GLA University's Entrepreneurship Cell, where I'm the point of contact between the ECell team and the wider student body, and a member of the AstroClub — proof that not everything I do is a strategy document.

03 — Ventures & Builds

Strategy and product, shipped.

Product · AI Shipping in progress

MediHintz

Health guidance & symptom-triage app

A health-guidance app that helps people understand symptoms and next steps without diagnosing or prescribing medication. I designed the core flow — symptom input by voice or text, an AI-assisted assessment, a color-coded risk result (green / yellow / red), and a nearby healthcare-facility finder — and the full UI/UX prototype in Figma, using a calm teal-and-orange visual identity and Google Stitch to accelerate screen generation. The red-flow path includes time-bound escalation nudges, check-in reminders, and a one-tap emergency call.

  • Progressively shipping the core flow as a Next.js app with a supporting API
  • Full prototype designed end-to-end in Figma
