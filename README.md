# Mayank Sharma — Portfolio

A personal portfolio site built around a "strategy brief" visual identity — presenting both sides
of my work: business strategy and applied AI/product building.

---

## About

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student at GLA University and Chief Strategy Officer at
Baoiam Innovations, an EdTech startup. This site is built to reflect that dual identity — someone
who designs go-to-market strategy and also ships the products that strategy is built for.

## What's on the site

- **Profile Brief (Hero)** — an ID-card-style introduction with role, location, and status
- **Highlights strip** — quantified numbers from my work: students engaged, GTM plans authored,
  target enrollments
- **About** — how strategy and building connect in my work
- **Experience** — a timeline of my roles: Chief Strategy Officer at Baoiam Innovations, Student
  Relations Member at GLA's Entrepreneurship Cell, AstroClub member, and my B.Tech at GLA University
- **Ventures & Builds** — case studies on:
  - **MediHintz** — an AI-assisted health-guidance and symptom-triage app (Next.js, TypeScript, Figma)
  - **Founder Batch GTM Strategy** — the go-to-market plan I led for Baoiam's AI-Ready Careers Program
  - **LinkedIn Content System** — a four-pillar personal-branding content strategy
  - Plus two industry job simulations (Tata Group and JPMorganChase, via Forage)
- **Capabilities** — skills grouped by Languages & Tools, Focus Areas, Design, and Business, plus
  a full list of certifications and programs
- **Contact** — email, phone, LinkedIn, and GitHub

## Tech stack

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework or build step required.

- **Fonts:** Fraunces (display), Inter (body), IBM Plex Mono (labels & data)
- **Interactivity:** mobile nav menu, scroll-spy navigation highlighting, scroll-reveal animations,
  animated metric counters, back-to-top button — all in vanilla JS (`script.js`)
- **Deployment-ready** for Vercel or GitHub Pages as a static site
